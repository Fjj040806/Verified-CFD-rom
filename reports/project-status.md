# Final release status

Version 1.0 closes the implemented project as a verified scientific-computing study and application-ready demonstration. The final report supersedes earlier next-stage plans.

Completed: MAC operators/projection; analytic and cavity verification; Re20 steady and Re100 periodic cylinder benchmarks; saved-data mass-weighted POD and affine DMD; chronological evaluation; cadence/count/window/phase controls; English academic manuscript, reproducibility guide, tested demonstration, curated figures and release archives.

Deferred research: systematic numerical-resolution effects on ROM, cross-Re prediction, cross-mesh transfer, global spatial convergence and a publishable originality claim. These are explicitly outside the completed release, not claimed results.

Publication status: no submitted or accepted paper and no established new algorithm. Repository: https://github.com/Fjj040806/Verified-CFD-rom. The expanded manuscript and analysis-ready project are published together.

Acceptance evidence is recorded in `release-validation.json`: numerical tests, quick-start reproduction and archive content checks. A lightweight package and full research archive serve different reproduction needs; each includes its own manifest.
